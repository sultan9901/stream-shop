How top-ups work
  1. Pick a package and send the exact BDT amount to the listed number.
  2. Upload the payment screenshot — this only opens a request.
  3. Status becomes PENDING VERIFICATION.
  4. A Master or authorised Seller checks it and confirms.
  5. Coins land in your wallet and you get a notification.

Uploading a screenshot never credits coins by itself, and a request can only ever be confirmed once — the ledger enforces it server-side.
